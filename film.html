Film Profiles

Las Sicodélicas

Year: 1968

Director: Gilberto Martínez Solares

Countries: Mexico, Peru

Genres: Black comedy, thriller

Main cast: Maura Monti, Amedée Chabot, Isela Vega, Elizabeth Campbell, Rogelio Guerra

Production: Filmadora Peruana, Producciones Rodríguez

Release date: August 29, 1968

General Description

Las Sicodélicas is a Mexican-Peruvian co-production from the late 1960s. The film combines black comedy, thriller elements, crime, pop aesthetics, and a playful tone associated with the visual culture of that decade. Its style reflects the influence of spy films, mod fashion, and the colorful, exaggerated atmosphere of 1960s genre cinema.

What It Is About

The story follows a group of young women connected to a criminal plot involving deception, murder, and social appearances. Behind an elegant and playful surface, the film develops a darker story about manipulation, money, and crime. Its characters move through a world of glamour and danger, where humor and suspense coexist.

Main Themes

Crime, disguise, black humor, femininity, social performance, pop culture, and the influence of international genre cinema.

Value for the Location Project

This film is useful for showing a less realistic and more stylized cinematic image of Lima and Peru. Instead of portraying the city through social realism, it connects Peru with a playful, international, and visually exaggerated film language.

Tarata

Year: 2009

Director: Fabrizio Aguilar

Country: Peru

Genre: Drama

Main cast: Gisela Valcárcel, Miguel Iza, Ricardo Ota, Silvana Cañote

Production: Luna Llena Films

Release date: September 1, 2009

General Description

Tarata is a Peruvian drama centered on the memory of the 1992 Tarata bombing in Miraflores. The film does not approach the event only as a political tragedy, but also as a domestic and urban fracture. It shows how political violence entered residential spaces and affected the everyday life of families in Lima.

What It Is About

The film follows a family from Miraflores during the years of internal violence in Peru. Through their daily routines, fears, family conflicts, and social environment, the story builds a portrait of a city living under tension. The bombing of Tarata becomes the central event that reveals how violence could reach even the most residential and apparently protected areas of the capital.

Main Themes

Political violence, family, fear, memory, terrorism, urban trauma, and the impact of national conflict on private life.

Value for the Location Project

This film has a direct relationship with Calle Tarata. The street is not only a filming reference, but also the symbolic and historical center of the film. It is one of the strongest links between a Peruvian film and a specific Lima location.

Gregorio

Year: 1984

Directors: Fernando Espinoza, Stefan Kaspar, Alejandro Legaspi

Country: Peru

Genre: Drama

Main cast: Marino León, Vetzy Pérez, Augusto Varillas

Production: Grupo Chaski

Release date: 1984

General Description

Gregorio is one of the most important films of Peruvian social cinema from the 1980s. Produced by Grupo Chaski, it portrays Lima through the experience of Andean migration, urban poverty, and childhood on the streets. The film is especially valuable because it presents the city from the point of view of a migrant child.

What It Is About

Gregorio is a boy who migrates with his family from the Andes to Lima. The city appears as a difficult and unequal place where the promise of a better life quickly becomes a struggle for survival. As the family faces poverty and instability, Gregorio begins to work on the streets and discovers a city marked by social contrast, informality, and exclusion.

Main Themes

Migration, childhood, poverty, informal work, social inequality, cultural displacement, and urban survival.

Value for the Location Project

The film can be connected to Parque Kennedy, Avenida Larco, and central areas of Miraflores as spaces of work, transit, and social contrast. Its value lies in showing Lima not as a tourist postcard, but as a city experienced from the street.

La Hora Final

Year: 2017

Director: Eduardo Mendoza de Echave

Country: Peru

Genres: Drama, police thriller

Main cast: Pietro Sibille, Nidia Bermejo, Toño Vega

Production: DAFO

Release date: September 1, 2017

General Description

La Hora Final is a Peruvian police thriller based on the intelligence operation that led to the capture of Abimael Guzmán, leader of Shining Path. The film reconstructs a crucial moment in Peru’s recent history through suspense, investigation, and political tension.

What It Is About

The story follows police agents involved in a secret investigation during the years of terrorism in Peru. Their work consists of surveillance, analysis, and patient observation rather than spectacular action. As they move through Lima, the film presents a city under suspicion, where houses, streets, universities, and ordinary neighborhoods can become part of a hidden political conflict.

Main Themes

Terrorism, police intelligence, surveillance, political violence, state investigation, fear, and historical memory.

Value for the Location Project

This film is useful for locations related to political memory, universities, residential districts, and hidden urban networks. It helps represent Lima as a city where the internal conflict was not only visible in public events, but also hidden inside domestic and institutional spaces.

Palomillas del Rímac

Year: 1938

Director: Sigifredo Salas

Country: Peru

Genres: Comedy, musical

Main cast: Edmundo Moreau, María Manuela, Jesús Vásquez

Production: Amauta Films

Release date: December 1, 1938

General Description

Palomillas del Rímac belongs to an early stage of Peruvian sound cinema. It is important because it preserves an image of old Lima connected to criollo music, neighborhood life, comedy, and popular traditions.

What It Is About

The film portrays the daily life of a family and its social environment in the district of Rímac. Through humor, music, and neighborhood situations, it presents a Lima where collective life, popular characters, and criollo culture are central. Music is not only decoration, but part of the identity of the place and its people.

Main Themes

Criollo culture, neighborhood life, popular comedy, music, family, and traditional Lima.

Value for the Location Project

This film connects strongly with the Rímac district, the Rímac River, and the Historic Center of Lima. It is useful for representing an older, musical, and popular image of the city.

¡Asu Mare!

Year: 2013

Director: Ricardo Maldonado

Country: Peru

Genres: Comedy, biographical film

Main cast: Carlos Alcántara, Emilia Drago, Carlos Carlín

Production: Tondero Producciones

Release date: April 11, 2013

General Description

¡Asu Mare! is a Peruvian biographical comedy based on the life and stand-up show of Carlos Alcántara. It became one of the most commercially successful Peruvian films of its time and helped reconnect national cinema with a mass audience.

What It Is About

The film follows Carlos “Cachín” Alcántara from his childhood and youth to his path toward becoming an actor and public figure. His relationship with his mother is one of the emotional centers of the story. Through humor, the film presents family life, neighborhood memories, economic difficulties, dreams of success, and the formation of a popular entertainer.

Main Themes

Family, humor, social mobility, neighborhood memory, popular culture, and personal ambition.

Value for the Location Project

The film is useful for representing everyday Lima: family neighborhoods, working-class and middle-class spaces, streets, homes, and places connected to personal memory.

Juliana

Year: 1989

Directors: Fernando Espinoza, Alejandro Legaspi

Country: Peru

Genre: Drama

Main cast: Rosa Isabel Morffino, Julio Vega

Production: Grupo Chaski

Release date: March 23, 1989

General Description

Juliana is a Peruvian social drama produced by Grupo Chaski. Like Gregorio, it focuses on childhood, poverty, and the street, but from the perspective of a girl. It is one of the most important Peruvian films about children living and working in Lima.

What It Is About

Juliana is a girl who runs away from home to escape abuse. On the streets, she discovers that surviving as a girl is especially difficult, so she cuts her hair and pretends to be a boy. She joins a group of children who sing on buses and are controlled by an adult who exploits them. Over time, Juliana becomes a figure of resistance and leadership among the children.

Main Themes

Street childhood, gender, domestic violence, exploitation, survival, rebellion, and social inequality.

Value for the Location Project

This film connects with streets, public transportation, markets, avenues, and informal urban spaces. It shows Lima as a place of danger and survival, especially for children excluded from family and institutional protection.

Alias “La Gringa”

Year: 1991

Director: Alberto Durant

Countries: Peru, Cuba, Spain, United Kingdom, France

Genres: Action, drama

Main cast: Germán González, Elsa Olivero

Production: Channel Four Films, ICAIC

Release date: 1991

General Description

Alias “La Gringa” is a Peruvian action drama with strong political and prison-related elements. The film is closely associated with El Frontón, an island prison off the coast of Callao. It uses the prison space as both a physical setting and a symbol of isolation, violence, and national conflict.

What It Is About

The story follows a fugitive known as “La Gringa,” who returns voluntarily to El Frontón prison to rescue a professor who once saved his life. Inside the prison, the characters face a violent and unstable environment marked by criminal codes, political prisoners, and institutional control. The island becomes an oppressive space where escape, loyalty, and survival define the story.

Main Themes

Prison, escape, loyalty, political violence, marginality, isolation, and the internal conflict in Peru.

Value for the Location Project

This film has a direct relationship with Isla El Frontón. It is one of the clearest cases where a location is central to the narrative, atmosphere, and historical meaning of the film.

Metal y Melancolía

Year: 1993

Director: Heddy Honigmann

Countries: Netherlands, Peru

Genre: Documentary

Participants: Lima taxi drivers

Production: Ariel Film Production

Release date: 1993

General Description

Metal y Melancolía is a documentary filmed in Lima during the economic crisis of the early 1990s. It portrays the city through taxi rides and conversations with drivers from different social and professional backgrounds. The film turns the taxi into a moving space of confession, memory, and survival.

What It Is About

The documentary follows different people who drive taxis to survive during a period of crisis. Many of them were once professionals, public employees, artists, or workers whose lives changed because of inflation and economic collapse. Through their stories, Lima appears fragmented through traffic, streets, car windows, voices, and memories.

Main Themes

Economic crisis, informal work, mobility, memory, urban melancholy, survival, and social fragmentation.

Value for the Location Project

The film can be connected to many Lima locations because its central location is the city in movement. It works especially well with avenues, downtown Lima, popular districts, residential areas, and places seen from the road.

Viejos amigos

Year: 2014

Director: Fernando Villarán Luján

Country: Peru

Genre: Comedy

Main cast: Ricardo Blume, Carlos Gassols, Enrique Victoria Fernández

Production: Mama Okllo Films

Release date: August 14, 2014

General Description

Viejos amigos is a Peruvian comedy about friendship, old age, memory, football, and Callao. The film combines humor and nostalgia to portray a group of elderly friends who reconnect with their past and with the port identity of their neighborhood.

What It Is About

Three elderly friends steal the urn containing the ashes of their deceased friend in order to fulfill his final wish: to have his ashes scattered in the sea near Callao. Their journey becomes an emotional and comic route through memories, old friendships, football passion, and the urban identity of the port.

Main Themes

Friendship, aging, memory, Callao identity, football, nostalgia, and loyalty.

Value for the Location Project

The film has a strong relationship with Callao Monumental, Playa Cantolao, the sea, and the emotional geography of the port. It is one of the most useful films for building a Callao itinerary.

Lima 13

Year: 2012

Director: Fabrizio Aguilar

Country: Peru

Genre: Drama

Main cast: Ubaldo Huamán, Norma Martínez

Production: Luna Llena Films

Release date: November 22, 2012

General Description

Lima 13 is a choral drama set in contemporary Miraflores. The title refers to the postal code of the district, making Miraflores part of the film’s identity. The film portrays an urban environment marked by loneliness, anxiety, surveillance, and emotional distance.

What It Is About

The story takes place during a period marked by fear of the end of the world. A security guard observes the lives of several people in a residential area. Through these characters, the film explores different forms of solitude, memory, fear, and human connection in a modern district of Lima.

Main Themes

Urban loneliness, surveillance, anxiety, modern life, emotional isolation, and residential Lima.

Value for the Location Project

This film relates strongly to Miraflores, Parque Kennedy, Larcomar, and the district’s residential and tourist axis.

Esta es la U

Year: 2024

Director: Daniel Farfán

Country: Peru

Genre: Sports documentary

Participants: Héctor Chumpitaz, Guillermo del Solar, José Luis Carranza, Edison Flores, Aldo Corzo, Eduardo Rey Muñoz, Antenor Guerra, Eloy Seclén, Juan Carlos Oblitas

Production: La Gorda Films

Release date: December 12, 2024

General Description

Esta es la U is a sports documentary dedicated to Universitario de Deportes, one of Peru’s most important football clubs. It was produced in the context of the club’s centenary and works as a tribute to its history, players, supporters, and collective identity.

What It Is About

The documentary follows the history and emotional universe of Universitario de Deportes through archival material, testimonies, football memories, and the passion of its supporters. It connects the club’s sporting achievements with its cultural role in Peruvian society.

Main Themes

Football, memory, fandom, sporting identity, community, tradition, and Peruvian popular culture.

Value for the Location Project

This documentary connects directly with Estadio Lolo Fernández, one of the most symbolic spaces in the history of Universitario. It can also relate to urban areas connected to football memory and fan culture.

Arde Lima

Year: 2023

Director: Alberto Castro

Country: Peru

Genre: Documentary

Participants: Tany de la Riva, Georgia Hart, La Langosta, Harmonik Minaj

Production: Arde Lima Cine

Release date: November 15, 2023

General Description

Arde Lima is a documentary about the Peruvian drag scene. It records artistic practices, personal stories, performance spaces, and forms of resistance within a city where diversity often has to fight for visibility.

What It Is About

The film presents the lives and performances of several drag artists in Lima. Through interviews, shows, memories, and urban nightlife, it explores drag as art, identity, community, and survival. The documentary also reflects on discrimination, visibility, and the construction of alternative cultural spaces.

Main Themes

Drag performance, LGBTQ+ culture, nightlife, identity, resistance, memory, and artistic expression.

Value for the Location Project

The film relates strongly to Calle de las Pizzas, Miraflores nightlife, bars, performance venues, and urban spaces connected to diversity and queer culture.

Caídos del Cielo

Year: 1990

Director: Francisco Lombardi

Countries: Peru, Spain

Genre: Drama

Main cast: Gustavo Bueno, Marisol Palacios

Production: Inca Films

Release date: November 1, 1990

General Description

Caídos del Cielo is a choral drama by Francisco Lombardi set in Lima during a period of crisis. The film presents different stories that reveal a city marked by inequality, frustration, decay, and social tension.

What It Is About

The film connects several characters whose lives are shaped by loneliness, economic difficulty, and emotional wounds. Through these stories, Lima appears as a fragmented city where chance, poverty, and desire intersect. The urban landscape becomes part of the film’s atmosphere of decline and uncertainty.

Main Themes

Social crisis, inequality, death, loneliness, urban decay, chance, and moral collapse.

Value for the Location Project

The film can be connected to the Miraflores Boardwalk, the Costa Verde, Playa Agua Dulce, and other locations that show Lima as a city of sharp social and visual contrasts.

Un mundo para Julius

Year: 2021

Director: Rossana Díaz Costa

Countries: Peru, Argentina, Spain

Genre: Drama

Main cast: Augusto Linares, Rodrigo Barba, Pamela Saco Polini, Fiorella de Ferrari, Mayella Lloclla, Nacho Fresneda, Camila Mac Lennan

Production: RTVE, VisionaTV, Tombuktú Films, Machaco Films

Release date: October 15, 2021

General Description

Un mundo para Julius is the film adaptation of Alfredo Bryce Echenique’s novel. It portrays Lima’s upper class through the eyes of a child who gradually becomes aware of the inequalities that structure his family and social world.

What It Is About

Julius is a child from a wealthy Lima family. He grows up surrounded by luxury, servants, strict social codes, and emotional distance. Through his innocent perspective, the film reveals the contradictions of an aristocratic world: affection and hierarchy, privilege and exclusion, beauty and cruelty.

Main Themes

Childhood, class inequality, aristocracy, domestic service, privilege, innocence, and social critique.

Value for the Location Project

The film relates strongly to Lima Golf Club, Bosque El Olivar, Country Club Lima Hotel, and residential San Isidro. These spaces visually represent the social world that the film observes and questions.

La Luz en el Cerro

Year: 2016

Director: Ricardo Velarde

Country: Peru

Genres: Thriller, mystery

Main cast: Manuel Gold, Emilram Cossío

Production: Caudal Films

Release date: October 1, 2017

General Description

La Luz en el Cerro is a Peruvian mystery thriller. Unlike many of the films in this list, it does not focus mainly on Lima’s social or historical spaces, but on atmosphere, suspense, landscape, and hidden tensions.

What It Is About

The film develops a story marked by mystery, fear, and investigation. Its title suggests a space where something strange or unexplained appears in the landscape. The narrative uses suspense and uncertainty to create a tense relationship between people, territory, and the unknown.

Main Themes

Mystery, fear, landscape, secrecy, investigation, local beliefs, and psychological tension.

Value for the Location Project

This film is useful for expanding the project beyond urban Lima. It can be connected to locations with a mysterious, rural, peripheral, or symbolic atmosphere.

Viaje a Tombuctú

Year: 2014

Director: Rossana Díaz Costa

Countries: Peru, Argentina

Genre: Drama

Main cast: Andrea Patriau, Jair García

Production: Tombuktú Films, Machaco Films

Release date: May 29, 2014

General Description

Viaje a Tombuctú is a Peruvian drama about adolescence, love, memory, and the loss of innocence during the 1980s. The film combines an intimate coming-of-age story with the broader context of terrorism, migration, and economic crisis in Peru.

What It Is About

Ana and Lucho are two teenagers who create an imaginary country called Tombuctú as a refuge from the violence and uncertainty around them. Their relationship becomes a private world where they can preserve affection, freedom, and innocence. However, the political and social reality of Peru gradually threatens that fragile space.

Main Themes

Adolescence, love, memory, terrorism, imagination, political violence, and the loss of innocence.

Value for the Location Project

The film connects strongly with Playa Cantolao and La Punta. The coastal landscape works as a space of memory, youth, nostalgia, and emotional transition.

Director: Óscar Kantor

Country: Peru

Genre: Drama

Main cast: Kiko Ledgard, Saby Kamalich, Patricia Aspíllaga, Enrique Victoria Fernández, Manie Rey, Gladys Arista

Production: Not specified

Release date: Not specified

General Description

El embajador y yo is a Peruvian film from the 1960s associated with a more elegant and urban image of Lima. Its cast includes figures linked to Peruvian entertainment, television, and popular culture of the period.

What It Is About

Although detailed public information about the plot is limited, the film can be read as part of a cinema interested in social intrigue, modern urban spaces, diplomacy, and refined environments. It belongs to a period in which Peruvian cinema also explored cosmopolitan and socially polished images of the capital.

Diplomacy, social life, urban elegance, modernity, intrigue, and Lima’s upper-class spaces.

Value for the Location Project

The film can be connected to San Isidro, Lima Golf Club, and areas associated with diplomatic, residential, and elite Lima in the 1960s.

Muero por Muriel

Year: 2007

Director: Augusto Cabada

Country: Peru

Genres: Drama, thriller

Main cast: Andrea Montenegro, Diego Bertie

Production: Iguana Producciones

Release date: 2007

General Description

Muero por Muriel is a Peruvian drama-thriller centered on emotional tension, desire, and conflict. It belongs to a group of Peruvian films from the 2000s that explored more intimate, psychological, and suspenseful stories.

What It Is About

The film revolves around a relationship marked by attraction, obsession, and emotional conflict. The title suggests that the character of Muriel occupies the center of the protagonist’s desire or crisis. The film can be read as a story where love, danger, and psychological tension become closely connected.

Main Themes

Desire, obsession, emotional conflict, psychological tension, love, and danger.

Value for the Location Project

This film can be linked interpretively to Barranco, Puente de los Suspiros, or other intimate and nocturnal spaces. The relationship should be presented as curatorial rather than as a confirmed precise location.

No se lo digas a nadie

Year: 1998

Director: Francisco Lombardi

Countries: Peru, Spain

Genre: Drama

Main cast: Santiago Magill, Christian Meier

Production: Inca Films

Release date: 1998

General Description

No se lo digas a nadie is a Peruvian drama based on the novel by Jaime Bayly. The film was significant because it openly addressed homosexuality, repression, and hypocrisy within Lima’s upper class.

What It Is About

The film follows Joaquín Camino, a young man from Lima’s wealthy social environment who struggles with his sexuality in a conservative and judgmental society. His family, school, religion, and social circle pressure him to hide his identity. Through his relationships and personal conflicts, the film exposes the contradictions of an elite that protects appearances while denying emotional truth.

Main Themes

Sexual identity, homophobia, upper-class hypocrisy, family repression, religion, secrecy, and social pressure.

Value for the Location Project

The film connects with Óvalo Gutiérrez, San Isidro, Miraflores, Lima Golf Club, and university or residential spaces associated with Lima’s upper and upper-middle classes.

Django: La Otra Cara

Year: 2002

Director: Ricardo Velásquez

Country: Peru

Genres: Action, crime

Main cast: Giovanni Ciccia, Melania Urbina

Production: Inca Films

Release date: June 26, 2002

General Description

Django: La Otra Cara is a Peruvian action and crime film inspired by the figure of Orlando Hernández, known as “Django,” a famous bank robber. The film helped establish a criminal saga within contemporary Peruvian cinema.

What It Is About

The film follows Django’s life as a criminal, lover, fugitive, and urban legend. Through robberies, betrayals, romantic relationships, and confrontations with the police, the story presents the world of crime as both dangerous and seductive. Lima appears as a city of banks, streets, prisons, and hidden networks.

Main Themes

Crime, robbery, masculinity, violence, love, betrayal, police pursuit, and urban myth.

Value for the Location Project

The film can be connected to Callao Monumental, port-related streets, closed urban spaces, and locations associated with crime, pursuit, and tension.

Django: Sangre de mi sangre

Year: 2018

Director: Aldo Salvini

Country: Peru

Genres: Action, crime, drama

Main cast: Giovanni Ciccia, Aldo Miyashiro, Melania Urbina, Emanuel Soriano, Tatiana Astengo, Sergio Galliani

General Description

Django: Sangre de mi sangre is the sequel to Django: La Otra Cara. It returns to the character of Django after years in prison and explores the possibility of redemption in a world where violence and crime continue to shape family life.

What It Is About

After being released from prison, Django tries to rebuild his life and reconnect with his family. However, the criminal world reappears through his son, who becomes involved with dangerous people. The film turns crime into an inherited conflict, where the father’s past threatens the next generation.

Main Themes

Crime, family, inheritance, redemption, prison, urban violence, and generational conflict.

Value for the Location Project

The film can be linked to Callao Monumental, urban crime spaces, enclosed streets, and locations associated with violence, pursuit, and family tension.

La última tarde

Year: 2016

Director: Joel Calero

Countries: Peru, Colombia

Genre: Drama

Main cast: Katerina D’Onofrio, Lucho Cáceres

Production: Habanero Films

Release date: 2016

General Description

La última tarde is an intimate Peruvian drama about memory, politics, love, and unresolved emotional wounds. The film is structured around a long conversation between two former partners who meet again after many years.

What It Is About

Laura and Ramón meet to finalize their divorce. As they walk and talk, their past as a couple and as political militants returns. Their conversation reveals old wounds, ideological differences, affection, resentment, and the traces left by Peru’s political violence in their private lives.

Main Themes

Memory, former love, divorce, political militancy, guilt, dialogue, and post-conflict reflection.

Value for the Location Project

The film connects well with Bosque El Olivar, Puente de los Suspiros, Barranco, and other walking spaces where the city becomes a place for remembering and confronting the past.

Cholo

Year: 1972

Director: Bernardo Batievsky

Country: Peru

Genre: Biographical film

Main cast: Hugo Sotil

Production: Procine S.A.

Release date: 1972

General Description

Cholo is a Peruvian biographical film starring footballer Hugo Sotil. It combines sports biography, fiction, and social commentary on migration, racism, and popular success.

What It Is About

The film follows a young man from the provinces who arrives in Lima and faces discrimination and hardship. His talent for football becomes a way to overcome social barriers and gain national recognition. Through his rise, the film explores the relationship between sport, class, race, and identity in Peru.

Main Themes

Football, migration, racism, social mobility, popular identity, talent, and national recognition.

Value for the Location Project

The film can be related to Estadio Lolo Fernández, Avenida Brasil, and other spaces connected to football culture and popular urban memory in Lima.
