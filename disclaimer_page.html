About Lima Live

Lima Live Museum of Movie Locations is a virtual museum dedicated to the places where Lima has appeared on screen. It brings together cinema, urban history and digital mapping to show how filmmakers have represented the Peruvian capital across different periods, neighbourhoods and social contexts.

The project treats a filming location as more than a backdrop. A street, square, beach, hotel, market or residential complex can preserve evidence of political conflict, migration, inequality, popular culture, everyday life and the changing image of the city.

Explore the collection

The current collection contains 29 locations and 29 films. Visitors can explore them through the interactive map, open detailed location records, compare film metadata and adapt introductory texts according to preferred length and complexity.

The Narratives section offers four curated perspectives:

  • Lima on Screen: a historical timeline following the cinematic city from 1938 to 2024.
  • Cinematic Miraflores: a route through Kennedy Park, Tarata Street, Larco Avenue, the boardwalk and Pizza Street.
  • San Isidro: a route connecting El Olivar Forest, Country Club Lima Hotel and the Santa Cruz Residential Complex.
  • Callao & La Punta: a route from Jorge Chávez International Airport to Monumental Del Callao, Cantolao Beach and the islands of Callao.

These routes connect individual films to broader themes such as memory, urban development, social difference, port identity and the relationship between Lima and the Pacific Ocean.

A changing visual language

The collection remains the same, but visitors can change the visual language through which it is presented. Alongside the original contemporary design, the theme selector reinterprets the museum through early print, a mid-century newspaper, a 1980s–90s magazine, an early web portal and a speculative interface for 2035.

This system turns graphic design into part of the exhibition: it demonstrates how typography, colour, texture and interaction influence the way cultural information is read.

Academic context

Lima Live was created as an end-of-course project for Information Modeling and Web Technologies, part of the Master’s Degree in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge at the University of Bologna, under the supervision of Prof. Fabio Vitali.

Use of Generative AI

OpenAI’s ChatGPT and Codex were used during development as technical and editorial assistants. Their use included support with code drafting and debugging, interface and theme experimentation, repository maintenance, consistency checks, documentation structure and language revision.

Generative AI was not treated as an authoritative or bibliographic source. Project data, film–location associations, coordinates, photographs, itineraries and final editorial decisions were reviewed by the authors, while factual claims remain linked to external sources where available. All AI-assisted outputs included in the project were manually evaluated, tested and adapted.

The published website does not use generative AI at runtime and does not send visitor input to an AI service. Final responsibility for the project remains with Alice Sgarlata and Adriana Villafuerte. A fuller account of AI use is available in the project documentation.

Sources and Disclaimer

Film, historical and geographic information is provided for educational and research purposes. The project links to external sources where available, but does not claim to offer a complete catalogue of films made in Lima or a real-time travel service.

Maps are based on Leaflet and OpenStreetMap data. Film records may include identifiers and metadata from Wikidata. Copyright and related rights in films, photographs, texts derived from external sources and other third-party material remain with their respective owners.

Before an in-person visit, users should independently verify transport, accessibility, opening conditions, costs, safety information and access restrictions. Complete photographic attribution and licence information is being consolidated before final publication.
