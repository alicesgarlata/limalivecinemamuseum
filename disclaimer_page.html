Disclaimer

The purpose of Lima Live Cinema Museum is to explore typographic and layout styles for specialised cultural visits as an end-of-course project for Information Modeling and Web Technologies, part of the Master’s Degree in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge at the University of Bologna, under the supervision of Prof. Fabio Vitali.

Film, historical and geographic information is provided for educational and research purposes. The project links to external sources where available, but does not claim to offer a complete catalogue of films made in Lima or a real-time travel service.

Sources and external services

Maps are based on Leaflet and OpenStreetMap data. Film records may include identifiers and metadata from Wikidata. Copyright and related rights in films, photographs, texts derived from external sources and other third-party material remain with their respective owners.

The publication of referenced material is not intended as an alternative to or replacement for its original source. Links to the sources associated with individual locations are provided in their extended records where available.

Image rights

Complete photographic attribution and licence information is being consolidated before final publication. Copyright and related rights in location photographs, film stills and other third-party images remain with their respective owners.

Visitor information

Before an in-person visit, users should independently verify transport, accessibility, opening conditions, costs, safety information and access restrictions. Routes and practical information are interpretive aids and may not reflect real-time conditions.
