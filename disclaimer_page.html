Disclaimers & About this Project

The purpose of this web site is to explore various types of typographic and visual elements that can be used to create a compelling and informative presentation of movie locations. The site is designed to provide users with an engaging and interactive experience, allowing them to discover iconic movie locations around the world through a combination of text, images, and maps. By utilizing different typographic styles and visual elements, the site aims to enhance the storytelling aspect of the movie locations and provide users with a deeper understanding of their significance in the context of the films they were featured in.

This web site was created as an end-of-course project for the "Information Modeling and Web Technologies" course on the Master's Degree in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge of the University of Bologna, under the supervision of prof. Fabio Vitali.
